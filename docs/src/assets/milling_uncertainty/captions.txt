These are output-uncertainty bands, not confidence intervals for a mean or simultaneous coverage. The outlet statistic is an instantaneous volume-weighted diameter median. Empty initial mill, geometric-volume grid (100 pivots, 6.54e-17 to 1e-3 m3), positive fixed-pivot daughter projection, and segmented CVODE BDF with reltol=1e-9, abstol=1e-14.
