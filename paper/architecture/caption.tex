\newcommand{\PBMArchitectureCaption}{PopulationBalanceModels.jl architecture.
(i) Physical terms compose a population equation for particles, cells or other
entities, with differential/integral contributions, sources and sinks. Direct
bin-integrated discretization selects grids and per-term methods; a moment
formulation selects a tracked basis and moment family. Compilation checks
compatibility and prepares fixed structure. Supported moment equations use
tracked moments analytically and the selected reconstruction only where needed;
DQMOM stores weights and weighted abscissas rather than raw moments.
Dashed side inputs denote optional population/bulk coupling through
\texttt{PBMSystem} and prescribed axial transport through \texttt{AxialDispersion}.
Neither addition is required for a standalone population.
The transient branch binds physical initial distributions or numerical states,
a time span and optional prescribed inputs through \texttt{OperatingHistory}. Initialization converts
eligible physical descriptions automatically. Automatic \texttt{prepare_run} binds
parameter-dependent numerical data and isolates working storage; rates still use
current integration inputs. \texttt{ForwardSolver} configures eligible transient
execution and is reusable in estimation and UQ; simple method/step calls remain
supported. Optional native controls address stability/admissibility, while
external checks address completion and saved states. Stationary problems instead
use numerical guesses, constraints and checked root solves; they do not inherit
transient operating-history or step-control semantics.
Both eligible branches feed observation and statistical interfaces. MAP and
local approximations use the declared inference-coordinate posterior. Optional
Gauss--Newton, full-Hessian Laplace and MCMC analyses retain their own checks;
stationary implicit sensitivities require a smooth isolated root.
Forward UQ reuses eligible solves and observables without requiring calibration
data: MC accepts joint input laws or posterior draws, while non-intrusive PCE
uses independent normal, uniform or beta reference coordinates and an explicit
physical-input map. Independent full-model validation checks surrogate error.
PCE means/covariances are analytical; output quantiles/events use polynomial
evaluations. Method/representation/derivative restrictions still apply.
(ii) Required dependencies, optional extension roles and separate development
and figure tools, checked against current declarations. MC/PCE add no backend
dependency. The density glyph is conceptual.}
