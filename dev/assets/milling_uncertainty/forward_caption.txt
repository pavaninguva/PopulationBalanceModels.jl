Forward comparison with Barrasso et al. (2013), DOI 10.1007/s12247-013-9155-0, Figure 7. (a,b) Instantaneous volume-weighted outlet median and mill hold-up at 3350/4923 RPM; symbols are published simulation markers extracted from the original vector PDF, not experimental data. (c,d) Signed package-minus-source differences at the 22 noninitial source times. The solid 100-pivot calculation uses exactly the UQ numerics: geometric volume pivots from 6.54e-17 to 1e-3 m3, integrated lognormal feed, 8-point positive fixed-pivot daughter quadrature, analytic fines, the pivot-volume CDF median and checked CVODE BDF with reltol=1e-9 and abstol=1e-14 kg. An initial inventory of one second of feed (7.4 g) is a declared reconstruction. Dotted/dashed curves refine to 400/800 pivots at fixed endpoint volumes, with the same observation rule. No kinetic parameters are fitted. Hold-up is close, but diameter startup and some later values differ: this is a partial reproduction, not verified equivalence to the original gPROMS implementation. Initial inventory and speed history differ between the two experiments; the numerical model is shared.
