SELECTED DATA EXTRACTION AND TRANSFORMATION

The frozen selected source rows came from the earlier complete Tang/Mitchell
paper extraction. This handover changes scope and data roles; it does not
redigitize the source graphs. source_selection.json records the original CSV
names and hashes, selected experiment and resulting row counts.

NUMBER PSDS
Tang Fig 11d native raster,2066x1640 pixels. Calibration: L0 atx1170, L50 atx2051;
ordinate0 aty1514, ordinate1 aty868. Colors: black74 min, red180, blue420,
teal660. Color-threshold tracing returns the median vertical position in each
raster column. A five-pixel median filter reduces marker-outline oscillation.
Panel labels and legends are excluded from the trace. Selected raw pixel
coordinates, raw medians and filtered medians are retained in
selected_psd_traces_source.csv; inspect them against Tang_Figure11.jpeg.

The raw plotted ordinate is retained. Linear-axis interpolation gives the
0.5 um reconstruction grid, with conditional unit-area normalization on either
2.5-25 or2.5-47.5 um. The cumulative trapezoidal integral gives the CDF. The
two windows are alternatives, not extra observations. Unresolved ends in
the nominal reconstruction are flagged and filled0 only when explicitly
missing; internal trace gaps are interpolated and their nearest-trace distance
is retained. Flat near-axis tails are not independently established physical
populations. +0.005-ordinate sensitivity summaries are graph-reconstruction
sensitivities, not experimental error bars.

CONCENTRATION
Tang Fig 12a native raster,2066x784 pixels; isothermal inverted-triangle series.
Manual marker centers are retained. Calibration: t0 atx153.5, t700 atx971;
c0-axis aty658.5, c20-axis aty73. The nominal marker-reading sensitivity is
plus/minus2 pixels, corresponding to about1.7 min and 0.068 g/L per axis.
This excludes laboratory error and model discrepancy.

The first visible concentration marker is known to represent the initial
condition. Its source graph time 0.428 min is retained, while model time is
explicitly set 0. All other model times equal their unrounded graph readings.
Shared/occluded early markers retain that flag. No artificial concentration
points are interpolated into the active fit dataset.

PREPARATION
prepare_data.py copies these fixed source values to the active CSVs and adds
explicit split/observation-block labels. validate_handover.py verifies expected
counts, source hashes, roles, normalization and quantiles. Neither script
implements or fits a PBM. The source PDFs and figure rasters remain available
for independent inspection. Numeric CSV precision exceeds graph-reading and
experimental accuracy.
