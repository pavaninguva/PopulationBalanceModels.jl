Uncertainty in continuous milling

The uncertainty question

A continuous cone mill breaks incoming material until fragments can pass its screen. If the breakage and screen parameters are uncertain, how uncertain is the size of the discharged material after a change in impeller speed? Can a small polynomial-chaos surrogate recover the result of a much larger Monte Carlo calculation?

We first examine the deterministic model developed by Barrasso et al. (2013), using their Figure 7 startup calculation. We then propagate the four uncertain inputs considered by Bhonsale et al. (2018) with Monte Carlo (MC) and non-intrusive polynomial chaos expansion (PCE). There is no parameter fitting in this example.

The forward comparison is a partial reproduction: mill hold-up is close, but the full outlet-diameter trajectory is not recovered. The UQ results below therefore describe the stated numerical reconstruction; they are not evidence that the original published trajectories have been reproduced. Numerical accuracy, agreement with the source, sampling error and surrogate error are checked separately.

The milling model

The internal coordinate is particle volume, $v$ in m³. The density $n(v,t)$ is a whole-mill number inventory per particle-volume interval, not a slurry concentration. Barrasso's feed–breakage–screen model, also used by Bhonsale, is

\[\frac{\partial n(v,t)}{\partial t} =F_{\mathrm{in}}(v)-F_{\mathrm{out}}(v,t) +\int_v^\infty b(v,y)S(y,t)n(y,t)\,\mathrm{d}y-S(v,t)n(v,t).\]

Here $F_{\mathrm{in}}$ and $F_{\mathrm{out}}$ are number-flow densities. Breakage selects parents at the rate

\[S(v,t)=\alpha\,\mathrm{RPM}(t) \left(\frac{v}{v_{\mathrm{ref}}}\right)^\gamma, \qquad v_{\mathrm{ref}}=2.23\times10^{-9}\;\mathrm{m^3}.\]

The daughter law is lognormal in the volume ratio $r=v/y$, centred at $\log r=-\log(2.68\times10^5)$ and truncated at $r=1$. It is an expected daughter-count density, normalized by its first volume moment:

\[\int_0^y v\,b(v,y)\,\mathrm{d}v=y.\]

Normalizing it to one daughter instead would lose parent material. The sub-first-pivot daughter number and volume are integrated analytically and leave as fines. Their material is included once in total discharge.

The classifier acts on net feed and breakage production, with $B(v,t)=\int_v^\infty b(v,y)S(y,t)n(y,t)\,\mathrm{d}y$:

\[F_{\mathrm{out}}(v,t) =(1-f_d(d(v)))\left(F_{\mathrm{in}}(v)+B(v,t)-S(v,t)n(v,t)\right),\]

\[f_d(d)=\begin{cases} 0,&d\le(1-\delta)d_{\mathrm{screen}},\\ \dfrac{d-(1-\delta)d_{\mathrm{screen}}}{\delta d_{\mathrm{screen}}}, &(1-\delta)d_{\mathrm{screen}}<d\le d_{\mathrm{screen}},\\ 1,&d>d_{\mathrm{screen}}, \end{cases} \qquad d(v)=\left(\frac{6v}{\pi}\right)^{1/3}.\]

This algebraic discharge law is not a first-order Death hazard. The shared model helper assembles IntegratedBirth, Breakage and an explicit classifier CustomTerm in a PBMModel. PBMSystem accumulates discharged material from the same fluxes.

First: check the forward calculation

Barrasso's Figure 7 shows startup of the outlet volume-weighted diameter median and the material held in the mill. The comparison uses $\delta=0.44$, $\alpha=8.82\times10^{-6}$ RPM⁻¹ s⁻¹, $\gamma=0.34$ and $\sigma=2.10$, without refitting. These are also the input means in the later UQ calculation. The graph starts near 7.4 g; its complete initial size distribution is unavailable. Setting the initial population to one second of feed supplies that mass and is an explicit reconstruction assumption.

From a checkout with the milling environment prepared:

include("examples/verification/milling_forward.jl")

Positive linear volume hats deposit daughter particles. For the same continuous birth density and parent-pivot evaluation, these follow algebraically from Barrasso's adjacent-volume allocation in Eqs. (14)–(17). That observation does not identify every detail of the original gPROMS cell-average implementation. Auxiliary bin edges are chosen to give the published arithmetic pivot centres. Refinements to 200, 400 and 800 pivots retain the endpoint volumes.

Tenfold tighter BDF tolerances change the 100-pivot median by less than $5\times10^{-6}$ µm. A separately assembled affine matrix exponential, using analytical truncated-lognormal daughter integrals, agrees within $4\times10^{-6}$ µm at the checked times. They do not establish equivalence to the authors' parent reconstruction, initial distribution, median calculation or full-precision fitted parameters. The remaining source mismatch cannot be attributed to time integration from this evidence, nor does it establish that the source numerics are incorrect.

Then: propagate uncertain inputs

Bhonsale studies the response to a speed reduction at 30 s. Each realization now starts empty and uses the separate geometric-grid configuration tabulated above. The quantity of interest remains the instantaneous outlet volume-weighted diameter median, not a median of accumulated discharged material. At exactly 30 s the outlet uses the new speed; the inventory is continuous. Two integrations meet there so no solver stage mixes the rates. The 1500 RPM transient is outside the two speeds in the forward comparison.

These are Bhonsale's Gaussian marginals. Independence is assumed because a joint dependence is not supplied. The Gaussian law has a tiny physically inadmissible tail; invalid draws are recorded as failures, never clipped or redrawn. A negative exponent alone is not invalid. No invalid input occurred in the retained ensembles. Analytical PCE moments concern the polynomial's full Gaussian extension, with the same input-law qualification.

The prepared positive fixed-pivot projection follows Kumar and Ramkrishna (1996). Bhonsale's printed second hat in Eq. (27) has inconsistent numerator/limit signs; the literal negative expression is not used. This does not establish what the authors implemented. Daughter preparation is rebuilt when $\sigma$ changes.

Both calculations use the ordinary package residual with external CVODE BDF. An exact diagonal change to per-pivot kilograms conditions its linear algebra; package states remain scaled counts. Relative/absolute tolerances are $10^{-9}$ / $10^{-14}$ kg. Invariant zero-retention classes that start at zero are eliminated. CVODE nonnegative constraints and saved-state checks remain active; no population is clipped. This external solve does not inherit native SSP or realizability guarantees. The algebraic classifier also requires a compatible startup/history; arbitrary initial states need not yield a nonnegative net outlet flux.

MC and PCE through the same interface

include("examples/milling_uncertainty.jl")
using PopulationBalanceModels

uq = MillingUncertainty.problem()
mc = propagate(uq, MonteCarlo(12_000; seed=2018255); max_solves=12_000)
summary = uq_summary(mc; covariance=false)

UQProblem supplies the scenario builder, checked segmented solver, common times and a SystemObservable for the median. One solve supplies an entire history. A PCE uses the same forward problem at regression inputs:

box = hcat(fill(-2.0, 4), fill(2.0, 4)) # standard-normal coordinates
training = pce_design(uq.inputs; n=12, seed=2018257, bounds=box)
validation = propagate(uq, MonteCarlo(512; seed=2018256); max_solves=512)
pce = fit_pce(uq, PolynomialChaos(1);
    design=training, validation=validation, max_solves=12)
moments = uq_summary(pce)
cheap_draws = evaluate_pce(pce, MonteCarlo(50_000; seed=2018259))

Degree one has five coefficients; degree two has fifteen. The first-degree fit uses 12 LHS points, following Bhonsale Figure 7's caption; its prose instead describes nine sigma-design points. A separate nine-point centre/axial design checks that choice. Degree two uses nine centre/axial points plus 23 LHS points. These declared designs are not recovered author samples. The bounded training box covers about 83% of the four-dimensional Gaussian probability; it does not truncate the propagated law. The 512 independent validation inputs also test outside that box.

Results and what the bands mean

Solid lines are propagated means; the grey dashed line is the nominal solution at the input means. Shading gives pointwise 2.5/97.5 percentiles. Dotted lines show the explicitly approximate mean ± 1.96 SD reference. PCE percentiles use 50,000 cheap surrogate evaluations and need not be symmetric. Negative surrogate predictions remain in the exports.

The speed reduction changes outlet production immediately, while held material adjusts subsequently. This explains a rapid response followed by relaxation; it need not follow a first-order residence-time law. The band describes uncertainty in a predicted median. It is neither the spread of particle sizes within one mill, a confidence interval on an estimated mean, nor simultaneous trajectory coverage. Empty-start medians are undefined at zero; curves begin at 0.001 s without artificial seeds or denominators.

Bhonsale's band prose refers to an F distribution while Eq. (7) uses a t critical value. Its degrees of freedom are unavailable, so the bands above use the explicitly defined conventions rather than invented statistical inputs. Individual MC, first-degree PCE and second-degree PCE figures show startup and a speed-change zoom on common scales.

At 31 s, MC gives a mean of 275.96 µm, SD 47.00 µm and mean MCSE 0.43 µm. PCE1 and PCE2 give means 279.35 and 275.46 µm, with independent validation RMSEs 11.37 and 5.34 µm. The 6,000-to-12,000 prefix mean changes by 0.38 µm. These assess finite-ensemble and surrogate errors, not agreement with the publication.

Numerical checks and limitations

The forward checks separate time integration and daughter quadrature from mesh/observation effects. Their configuration and source record retains the graph-extraction method and reconstruction assumptions.

The retained UQ configuration has its own numerical checks. At 31 s, tighter time tolerances change the nominal median by $4.2\times10^{-7}$ µm; doubling daughter quadrature changes it by $5.4\times10^{-12}$ µm. Refining 100 to 200/400 pivots changes it by 6.18/9.50 µm; a fixed-spacing domain extension changes it by $1.8\times10^{-8}$ µm. Thus the 100-pivot UQ reference is not spatially converged to sub-micrometre precision. Its discretization effect exceeds the reported PCE2 validation RMSE. Across production ensembles, the maximum material defect is $7.3\times10^{-12}$ kg, saved populations are nonnegative and upper-pivot material is negligible. Conservation does not establish continuum accuracy.

Complete script

The displayed model includes the feed distribution, volume-additive daughter measure and its conservative projection, breakage law, algebraic screen, material accounting and instantaneous outlet median. The UQ declaration then shows the four-input map, scenario rebuild, solver and output meaning.

The one external include is the checked numerical BDF adapter, not a hidden physical model: it advances the shown package residual in mass coordinates and restarts exactly at the 30-s speed change. Its complete source is provided separately. Run in the milling environment prepared above.

The small demonstration uses 40 pivots, 32 MC solves, 16 validation solves and 12/32 PCE training solves: 92 full solves, rather than the production 12,000-realization study. The much cheaper surrogate draws require no PBM solves.

Download this standalone script. Run it from a checkout with the package developed into the selected environment:

julia --project=examples/environments/milling docs/src/assets/source/tutorials/milling_uncertainty.jl

The code is assembled from marked sections of the maintained examples; those same definitions are used by the reproduction drivers above. Model equations and assumptions have not been changed for this presentation.

# Minimal milling uncertainty tutorial.
# Generated from maintained scientific source sections and a short run block.
# No benchmark exports or retained files are written. See the case-study page.

using PopulationBalanceModels
using LinearAlgebra
import Distributions

# From examples/support/milling_model.jl (model).
module MillingModel
using PopulationBalanceModels, LinearAlgebra
import Distributions

const PARAMETER_NAMES=(:delta,:alpha,:gamma,:sigma)
const MEAN=(delta=.44,alpha=8.82e-6,gamma=.34,sigma=2.10)
const SD=(delta=.07,alpha=1.01e-6,gamma=.08,sigma=.12)
const GL8=PopulationBalanceModels._gauss_legendre(GaussLegendre(8))

parameters()=(mass_feed=.0074,rho=740.,feed_median=4.81e-5,feed_log_sd=.25,
    first_pivot=6.54e-17,last_pivot=1e-3,xref=2.23e-9,daughter_ratio=2.68e5,
    screen=1575e-6,change_time=30.,speed_before=4923.,speed_after=1500.,number_scale=1e12)
admissible(p)=all(isfinite,values(p)) && 0<p.delta<1 && p.alpha>0 && p.sigma>0
speed(p,t)=p.segment_speed==0 ? (t<p.change_time ? p.speed_before : p.speed_after) : p.segment_speed
diameter(v)=cbrt(6v/π)

"""Truncated raw moment of the relative lognormal daughter law on (0, upper).
The first moment at upper=1 normalizes expected daughter COUNT, not probability.
"""
function relative_moment(k,upper,sigma,ratio)
    upper<=0 && return 0.
    mu=-log(ratio)
    exp(k*mu+k^2*sigma^2/2)*Distributions.cdf(Distributions.Normal(),(log(upper)-mu-k*sigma^2)/sigma)
end

function hat_integral(lo,hi,sigma,ratio,rising,rule)
    hi<=lo && return 0.
    midpoint=(lo+hi)/2;half=(hi-lo)/2
    norm=relative_moment(1,1.,sigma,ratio)
    sum(eachindex(rule.nodes)) do i
        z=midpoint+half*rule.nodes[i]
        fraction=expm1(z-lo)/expm1(hi-lo)
        phi=rising ? fraction : 1-fraction
        phi>=0 || error("negative fixed-pivot hat weight")
        half*rule.weights[i]*phi*exp(-.5*((z+log(ratio))/sigma)^2)/(sigma*sqrt(2π)*norm)
    end
end

struct MillingDaughters
    pivots::Vector{Float64}
    eta::Matrix{Float64}
    underflow_number::Vector{Float64}
    underflow_volume::Vector{Float64}
    sigma::Float64
end
function (d::MillingDaughters)(parent,p,t,ctx)
    j=searchsortedfirst(d.pivots,parent)
    j<=length(d.pivots) && d.pivots[j]==parent || throw(ArgumentError("milling daughters are prepared at the fixed pivots"))
    pairs=[(d.pivots[i],d.eta[i,j]) for i in 1:j if d.eta[i,j]>0]
    if d.underflow_number[j]>0
        push!(pairs,(d.underflow_volume[j]/d.underflow_number[j],d.underflow_number[j]))
    end
    pairs
end

"""Positive standard hat projection; analytic sub-grid moments leave as fines.
Geometric pivots make the interior coefficients depend on the pivot offset.
This is preparation, not an alternative PBM time integrator.
"""
function daughter_projection(grid,sigma,ratio;order=8)
    x=grid.centers;n=length(x);h=log(x[end]/x[1])/(n-1)
    rule=order==8 ? GL8 : PopulationBalanceModels._gauss_legendre(GaussLegendre(order))
    left=[hat_integral((k-1)*h,k*h,sigma,ratio,true,rule) for k in 0:-1:-(n-1)]
    right=[k==0 ? 0. : hat_integral(k*h,(k+1)*h,sigma,ratio,false,rule) for k in 0:-1:-(n-1)]
    eta=zeros(n,n)
    for j in 1:n,i in 1:j
        index=j-i+1
        eta[i,j]=right[index]+(i==1 ? 0. : left[index])
    end
    norm=relative_moment(1,1.,sigma,ratio)
    low0=[relative_moment(0,x[1]/parent,sigma,ratio)/norm for parent in x]
    low1=[parent*relative_moment(1,x[1]/parent,sigma,ratio)/norm for parent in x]
    all(>=(0),eta) || error("negative daughter projection coefficient")
    defect=vec(x'*eta)+low1-x
    maximum(abs.(defect)./x)<2e-12 || throw(NumericalFailure("daughter quadrature does not resolve volume conservation; increase its order"))
    MillingDaughters(copy(x),eta,low0,low1,sigma)
end

struct MillingHazard
    alpha::Float64
    gamma::Float64
    xref::Float64
end
(rate::MillingHazard)(x,p,t,ctx)=rate.alpha*speed(p,t)*(x/rate.xref)^rate.gamma
struct MillingFixedPivot <: AbstractTermMethod end
function PopulationBalanceModels.prepare_term(term::Breakage,::MillingFixedPivot,grid::Grid1D;kwargs...)
    term.daughters isa MillingDaughters || throw(ArgumentError("MillingFixedPivot requires prepared milling daughters"))
    term.overflow===:discard || throw(ArgumentError("sub-grid milling daughters must be explicitly accounted as discharged fines"))
    selection=term.rate.alpha.*(grid.centers./term.rate.xref).^term.rate.gamma
    Q=(term.daughters.eta-Matrix{Float64}(I,length(grid),length(grid)))*Diagonal(selection)
    (;Q,work=zeros(length(grid)),low0=term.daughters.underflow_number.*selection,
      low1=term.daughters.underflow_volume.*selection,
      frozen=(term.rate.alpha,term.rate.gamma,term.daughters.sigma))
end
function PopulationBalanceModels.add_term!(du,N,term::Breakage,::MillingFixedPivot,cache,op,p,t,ctx)
    cache.frozen==(p.alpha,p.gamma,p.sigma) || throw(ArgumentError("rebuild the milling operator after changing kinetic parameters"))
    w=speed(p,t)
    # Match the algebraic screen's feed + w*(Q*N) arithmetic. BLAS's fused
    # alpha/beta form rounds differently and leaves a spurious derivative in
    # classes whose exact retention is zero.
    mul!(cache.work,cache.Q,N)
    du .+=w.*cache.work
    w*dot(cache.low0,N),w*dot(cache.low1,N)
end

function normal_interval(dist,a,b)
    a>=b && return 0.
    a>Distributions.median(dist) ? Distributions.ccdf(dist,a)-Distributions.ccdf(dist,b) :
        Distributions.cdf(dist,b)-Distributions.cdf(dist,a)
end
function grid(;bins=100,first=parameters().first_pivot,last=parameters().last_pivot)
    ratio=(last/first)^(1/(bins-1))
    Grid1D((2first/(1+ratio)).*ratio.^(0:bins);coordinate=:volume)
end
function retention(grid,p)
    d=diameter.(grid.centers);critical=(1-p.delta)*p.screen
    [x<=critical ? 0. : x>=p.screen ? 1. : (x-critical)/(p.delta*p.screen) for x in d]
end

function net_production(p,N,t)
    p.feed + speed(p,t)*(p.Q*N)
end
function outlet(p,N,t)
    produced=net_production(p,N,t)
    resolved=(1 .-p.retained).*produced
    low0=speed(p,t)*dot(p.low0,N)
    low1=speed(p,t)*dot(p.low1,N)
    (;resolved,underflow_number=low0,underflow_volume=low1)
end

"""Instantaneous outlet volume-weighted diameter median. The resolved CDF is
linearly interpolated in diameter between cumulative fixed-pivot volume values.
Sub-grid volume is included; an empty outlet is undefined, never seeded.
"""
function outlet_median(p,N,t)
    q=outlet(p,N,t);weights=p.pivots.*q.resolved
    all(>=(0),weights) && q.underflow_volume>=0 || throw(NumericalFailure("negative instantaneous outlet flux"))
    total=sum(weights)+q.underflow_volume
    total>0 || throw(NumericalFailure("outlet median is undefined before particles leave the mill"))
    target=total/2
    if target<q.underflow_volume
        # Resolve the continuous fines rather than inventing a first-bin median.
        lo=0.;hi=first(p.pivots);norm=relative_moment(1,1.,p.sigma,p.daughter_ratio)
        for _ in 1:65
            mid=(lo+hi)/2
            volume=speed(p,t)*sum(p.selection[j]*N[j]*p.pivots[j]*
                relative_moment(1,mid/p.pivots[j],p.sigma,p.daughter_ratio)/norm for j in eachindex(N))
            volume<target ? (lo=mid) : (hi=mid)
        end
        return 1e6diameter((lo+hi)/2)
    end
    cumulative=q.underflow_volume
    previous_d=1e6diameter(first(p.pivots))
    for i in eachindex(weights)
        next=cumulative+weights[i];d=1e6diameter(p.pivots[i])
        if next>=target
            return weights[i]>0 ? previous_d+(d-previous_d)*(target-cumulative)/weights[i] : d
        end
        cumulative=next;previous_d=d
    end
    throw(NumericalFailure("outlet median did not lie in the declared size domain"))
end

"""Published growth-free cone-mill equations, on an explicit reconstruction grid.
N is total particle inventory divided by number_scale (unit reference process
volume); rho*x*N*number_scale is kg, not a suspension concentration.
"""
function build(theta=MEAN;bins=100,tf=200.,last_pivot=parameters().last_pivot,quadrature_order=8)
    admissible(theta) || throw(NumericalFailure("milling inputs require 0<delta<1, alpha>0 and sigma>0"))
    constants=parameters();g=grid(;bins,last=last_pivot)
    (1-theta.delta)*constants.screen>=diameter(first(g.centers)) ||
        throw(NumericalFailure("the first milling pivot must lie below the freely discharged screen range; reduce the lower cutoff"))
    daughters=daughter_projection(g,theta.sigma,constants.daughter_ratio;order=quadrature_order)
    rate=MillingHazard(theta.alpha,theta.gamma,constants.xref)
    selection=theta.alpha.*(g.centers./constants.xref).^theta.gamma
    Q=(daughters.eta-Matrix{Float64}(I,bins,bins))*Diagonal(selection)
    distribution=Distributions.LogNormal(log(constants.feed_median),constants.feed_log_sd)
    fractions=[normal_interval(distribution,g.edges[i],g.edges[i+1]) for i in 1:bins]
    resolved_feed_fraction=sum(fractions)
    feed=constants.mass_feed/constants.rho .* (fractions/resolved_feed_fraction)./g.centers/constants.number_scale
    p0=merge(constants,theta,(;pivots=g.centers,edges=g.edges,selection,Q,
        low0=daughters.underflow_number.*selection,low1=daughters.underflow_volume.*selection,
        feed,segment_speed=0.,resolved_feed_fraction))
    p=merge(p0,(retained=retention(g,p0),))
    feedterm=IntegratedBirth((a,b,p,t,c)->p.feed[searchsortedfirst(p.edges,a)])
    # Classification is an algebraic removal of feed + net breakage production,
    # not an arbitrary first-order Death term. Recompute it explicitly so term
    # ordering does not determine the physical equation.
    screen=CustomTerm((du,N,g,p,t,c)->(du .-=outlet(p,N,t).resolved;nothing))
    model=PBMModel(feed=feedterm,breakage=Breakage(rate,daughters;overflow=:discard),classification=screen)
    op=compile(model,g;methods=TermMethods(by_term=(breakage=MillingFixedPivot(),)))
    balance=function(ctx,p,t)
        q=outlet(p,ctx.populations.mill,t)
        (discharged_mass=p.rho*p.number_scale*(dot(p.pivots,q.resolved)+q.underflow_volume),
         fines_mass=p.rho*p.number_scale*q.underflow_volume)
    end
    system=PBMSystem((mill=op,);bulk_rhs=balance,nonnegative_bulk=(:discharged_mass,:fines_mass))
    PBMProblem(system,(mill=zeros(bins),),(0.,tf);bulk0=(discharged_mass=0.,fines_mass=0.),p)
end

median_observable()=SystemObservable((state,op,p,t)->outlet_median(p,state.populations.mill,t))

"""Analytic Jacobian of the same affine residual, for external linear algebra
and independent matrix-exponential verification. Feed is the affine offset.
"""
function linear_system(pb)
    p=pb.p;n=length(p.pivots);w=speed(p,first(pb.tspan))
    A=zeros(n+2,n+2);A[1:n,1:n]=Diagonal(p.retained)*(w*p.Q)
    A[n+1,1:n]=p.rho*p.number_scale*w*(vec(p.pivots'*Diagonal(1 .-p.retained)*p.Q)+p.low1)
    A[n+2,1:n]=p.rho*p.number_scale*w*p.low1
    b=vcat(p.retained.*p.feed,p.rho*p.number_scale*dot(p.pivots,(1 .-p.retained).*p.feed),0.)
    A,b
end

# From examples/milling_uncertainty.jl (uq).
function output_times()
    sort!(unique(vcat(.001,.01,collect(.1:.1:2),collect(3.:10.),collect(12.:2:28.),
        29.,29.9,29.999,30.,30.001,30.1,30.5,31.,collect(32.:2:60.),
        collect(65.:5:100.),collect(110.:10:200.))))
end

physical(z) = NamedTuple{MillingModel.PARAMETER_NAMES}(Tuple(
    getproperty(MillingModel.MEAN,k)+getproperty(MillingModel.SD,k)*getproperty(z,k)
    for k in MillingModel.PARAMETER_NAMES))

function inputs()
    marginals = NamedTuple{MillingModel.PARAMETER_NAMES}(ntuple(_->Distributions.Normal(),4))
    UncertainInputs(marginals;map=physical,valid=MillingModel.admissible,
        coordinates=:standard_normal,metadata=(transformation=:table1_affine,
        joint_law=:independent_gaussian,physical_units=(:dimensionless,:inverse_RPM_seconds,
        :dimensionless,:dimensionless)))
end

function state_diagnostics(pb,sol)
    p=pb.p
    material=[p.rho*p.number_scale*dot(p.pivots,population(pb,u,:mill)) for u in sol.u]
    errors=[material[i]+bulk(pb,sol.u[i],:discharged_mass)-p.mass_feed*t for (i,t) in enumerate(sol.t)]
    occupancy=[p.rho*p.number_scale*p.pivots[end]*last(population(pb,u,:mill)) for u in sol.u]
    (;steps=sol.steps,minimum_inventory=minimum(minimum.(sol.u)),
        maximum_mass_error_kg=maximum(abs,errors),
        maximum_upper_pivot_mass_kg=maximum(occupancy),
        final_fines_mass_kg=bulk(pb,last(sol.u),:fines_mass))
end

"""Build a data-free forward UQ problem. Rebuild daughter projections when sigma
changes. All requested times share one segmented forward solve per realization.
"""
function problem(;bins=100,times=output_times(),reltol=1e-9,abstol=1e-14,
        quadrature_order=8,records=nothing,progress=0)
    base=MillingModel.build(;bins,quadrature_order)
    setup=(theta,base)->MillingModel.build(theta;bins,quadrature_order)
    calls=Ref(0)
    solver=function(pb,ts)
        calls[]+=1
        if progress>0 && (calls[]==1 || calls[]%progress==0)
            println("  forward solve ",calls[]);flush(stdout)
        end
        sol=MillingSolver.solve(pb,ts;reltol,abstol)
        records===nothing || push!(records,state_diagnostics(pb,sol))
        sol
    end
    UQProblem(base,inputs();setup,solver,times,outputs=(d50=MillingModel.median_observable(),),
        output_coordinates=(d50=(units=:micrometre,weighting=:instantaneous_outlet_volume,
            interpolation=:linear_diameter_CDF),))
end

function tutorial_problem(; bins=40)
    times = [1.0,5.0,29.9,30.0,31.0,60.0,200.0]
    problem(; bins, times)
end

function main()
    uq = tutorial_problem()
    # Demonstration budgets, not the retained 12,000-realization production run.
    mc = propagate(uq, MonteCarlo(32; seed=2018255); max_solves=32)
    validation = propagate(uq, MonteCarlo(16; seed=2018256); max_solves=16)
    box = hcat(fill(-2.0,4), fill(2.0,4)) # Training design, not a truncated input law.
    @show uq_summary(mc; covariance=false).mean.d50
    for (degree, count, seed) in ((1,12,2018257),(2,32,2018258))
        design = pce_design(uq.inputs; n=count, seed, bounds=box)
        pce = fit_pce(uq, PolynomialChaos(degree);
            design, validation, max_solves=count)
        cheap = evaluate_pce(pce, MonteCarlo(10_000; seed=2018259))
        @show degree pce.validation uq_summary(pce).mean.d50
        @show uq_summary(cheap; covariance=false).quantiles.d50
    end
end

if abspath(PROGRAM_FILE) == @__FILE__
    main()
